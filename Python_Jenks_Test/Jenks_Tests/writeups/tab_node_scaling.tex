\begin{table}[t]
  \centering
  \caption{GF4 codebook-decode area as a fraction of the FP4 PE, synthesized
  (Yosys) against open standard-cell libraries. \textbf{(a)} The hardwired
  Gaussian-quantile decode is $\approx\!1.5\%$ of the PE and node-invariant, and
  falls with datapath width (\texttt{OPERAND\_WIDTH}: 4 = narrow, 16 = W4A16).
  \textbf{(b)} Shared across a weight-stationary row of $N$ MAC lanes, the same
  decode amortizes as a clean $1/N$ (45\,nm, \texttt{OPERAND\_WIDTH}=8). At
  45\,nm the absolute decode is $14.1\,\mu\text{m}^2$ / $14$\,fJ per op inside a
  $963\,\mu\text{m}^2$ PE; energy tracks area for standard-cell logic at fixed
  activity, so the decode is likewise $<\!1\%$ of PE energy at every node. The
  regular E2M1 weight decode is smaller ($\approx\!0.6\%$), the $\approx\!0.9$\,pp
  gap being the price of GF4's scattered-bit Gaussian constants.}
